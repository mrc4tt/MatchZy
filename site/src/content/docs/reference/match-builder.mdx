Fill in the form and copy the match config, or download it as `match.json`. Load it with `matchzy_loadmatch_url` from your panel, or put it in `csgo/cfg/MatchZy/` and use `matchzy_loadmatch`. Every field is explained in [Match config JSON](/reference/match-config/). **Import** loads an existing match config to edit it. The problems the plugin would warn about when loading (a player on both teams or also a spectator, a roster larger than the team size) show above the result. Your form is kept in this browser. Nothing leaves your browser.
