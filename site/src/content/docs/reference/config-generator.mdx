Every setting in `cfg/MatchZy/config.cfg`, with its explanation and default. Search, change the values you need, and copy the result: your whole `config.cfg` with the defaults and your changes (marked), or only the changed lines to paste into `config.cfg` or into a private cfg that is executed after it. **Import** loads the values of the `config.cfg` you have now. Your changes are kept in this browser. The list is built from the plugin's own `config.cfg`, so it always matches this version. Nothing leaves your browser.

Every setting, including the ones that are not in `config.cfg`, is in [Configuration (convars)](/reference/convars/).
