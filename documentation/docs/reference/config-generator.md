# Settings generator

Change the settings you need, then copy your `config.cfg`. Nothing leaves your browser.

??? note "How it works"
    - The list is read from the plugin's own `cfg/MatchZy/config.cfg`, with each setting's explanation and default, so it always matches this version. Search by name or text, or jump to a section.
    - On the right, **Full config.cfg** is the whole file with your changes marked, and **Only changed** is only the lines you changed, to paste into `config.cfg` or into a private cfg that is executed after it.
    - **Import** loads the `config.cfg` you have now. Your changes are kept in this browser until you press **Reset all**.
    - Settings that are not in `config.cfg` are in [Configuration (convars)](convars.md).
